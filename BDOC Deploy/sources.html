Sources & Licenses

Last updated: November 5, 2026 · Kitsune Global Solutions LLC

BDOC puts public and licensed data on one globe. We don't own that data; the people below collect it, and they deserve the credit. Each source keeps its own terms, which apply to anything you take from BDOC. Map credits also appear at the bottom of the globe while a layer is on.

Something missing, wrong, or yours? Email support@kitsuneglobalsolutions.com and we'll fix it.

Software

BDOC is built on open-source software, including CesiumJS (Apache 2.0), milsymbol by Måns Beckman (MIT), which draws the MIL-STD-2525 / APP-6 unit symbols in the Battle Tracker and on the military layers, and the libraries served from cdnjs and jsDelivr under their own licenses. Payments are handled by Stripe, accounts by Supabase, hosting by Netlify.
